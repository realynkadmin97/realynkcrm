# Realynk brand bible

Source files in this folder:

- `realynk-identity-manual.pdf` — Branding Guidelines, Identity Manual (December 2024).
- `logo-realynk-assistants.png` — wordmark lockup on black: the blue mark, REALYNK, and ASSISTANTS.

The CRM screens still use their own type and teal palette. Use this file when a surface should follow the company identity.

## About

Realynk is a virtual assistance company that helps founders, executives, and thought leaders reach their zone of genius. The name joins "real" and "link": a real connection with a solution-driven approach.

## Mission

Empower founders, executives, and thought leaders with personalized Filipino virtual assistance, so they can stay in their zone of genius.

## Vision

Be the virtual assistance agency people associate with affordability, high quality, and transparent solutions.

## Core values

- **Transparency.** Open and honest without being asked. Accountability, integrity, and being proactive.
- **Solution-centered.** Fewer excuses. Sustainable, practical solutions.
- **Partnership.** Trust, respect, and shared success with the team, clients, and the community.

## Voice

Friendly and solution-oriented. Build the relationship with open communication, transparency, and a collaborative tone.

## Type

Lato. Clean, neutral, and flexible. Use it when a surface is meant to carry the brand.

## Imagery

- Photographs of real people working together.
- Gradient backgrounds when the layout should feel modern.
- Simple rounded icons and shapes, in two brand blues.

## Logo

Use the lockup in `logo-realynk-assistants.png` when the mark and the Realynk Assistants name should appear together. Keep the wordmark intact. Do not recolor the letters outside the blues above.

## Rea

Rea is the Realynk AI assistant. The mascot lives in `public/brand/rea-mascot.png`. The glass login sample is at `/landing/rea`. That page is a mockup and does not collect a password.

Default door line: **I'm Rea, Realynk's command center manager.** Supporting sentence: *Ask your admin for an invite if you're new here.*

## Waves

Soft intersecting ribbons use `#0543b8`, `#0257b5`, and `#022f7f`. On the Rea door they stay light and quiet so Realynk blue is an accent, not the whole page. Inspiration for that airy layout: `brand/fantom-inspiration.png` (Fantom). The FlowPro-style wave reference is `brand/wave-reference-flowpro.png`.
